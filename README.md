# Anegh's Tales

In Chapter 6 at Waukeen's Promenade, or in Amkethran, a mage named Nomoran will approach you and ask for your help in saving his village from a horde threatening to destroy it.

**The mod includes:**
- 3 new areas, including a village.
- A main quest and around ten side quests.
- NPCs and merchants.
- A large-scale battle.
- Plenty of new items to buy or find.
